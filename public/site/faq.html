FAQ

Questions & answers

Can't find what you need? Contact us.

Do I really not need a phone number?

Yes. Raazdar never asks for a phone number, email, name or contacts. When you sign up, your phone creates a random ID and a set of encryption keys. That ID is all anyone, including us, ever sees.

Can Raazdar read my messages?

No. Messages are encrypted on your phone and can only be decrypted on the recipient's phone. Our server only ever holds sealed envelopes it cannot open, and it deletes them once they're delivered (or after 7 days).

What happens if I forget my passcode?

Nobody can recover it, not even us. That's the price of real privacy. Keep an encrypted backup (You → Encrypted backup) somewhere safe; you can restore it with your passcode on any device.

Is it free?

Yes. Raazdar has no ads and does not sell or share data. There's nothing to sell.

How is this different from other private messengers?

Most messengers still tie your account to a phone number, and some now offer number-free sign-up only as a paid or beta option. Raazdar has never needed one, and it's free. It also hides the sender from the server (sealed sender), uses post-quantum encryption, works over Tor, and adds protection for people at risk: a panic wipe, duress passcode, auto-erase, calculator disguise and a decoy account.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone, open Raazdar in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. On Android, install the app or use the browser. It also works on any computer. See Download.

What does the panic button do?

Holding the lock button for two seconds (or typing your duress passcode) erases your keys, chats and settings from the device instantly, and can tell your contacts' apps to erase your conversations too. It cannot be undone.

Who can see that I use Raazdar?

On the normal web address, your internet provider can see you connect to raazdar.app (not what you send). For maximum anonymity use our Tor address, which hides your IP address from everyone, including us. See Security.

Is Raazdar legal to use in India?

Yes. Private, encrypted communication is lawful. Using Raazdar for anything illegal is not, and is prohibited by our Terms. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Act, 2000.

How do you make money without selling data?

From organisations, not from you. Companies, law firms and newsrooms pay for private Raazdar servers and our anonymous whistleblower channel. The personal app stays free, with no ads, ever. See how we make money.

How do I move Raazdar to a new phone?

Open You → Move to a new device. Scan the QR code (or open the link) on the new phone and enter your passcode. The link works once and expires in 10 minutes. Then erase the old phone.

Will I get notifications when the app is closed?

Yes, if you turn them on (You → Notifications). They only ever say “New activity”, never a name or message. On iPhone, add Raazdar to your Home Screen first.

Are calls encrypted? Can the other person see my IP address?

Calls are end-to-end encrypted. The call is set up inside your encrypted chat, so nobody can slip into the middle, and the audio and video are encrypted between the two phones. Every call goes through our relay, so neither of you learns the other's IP address. The relay can't hear or see the call and keeps no logs. Calls are switched off on the Tor address, because a call can't travel over Tor without revealing your IP.

Can someone hack my phone by sending me a photo, video or invitation?

Photos and videos can't run programs by themselves. Scams like fake wedding invitations work by sending an app (an .apk file) dressed up as a card or PDF, or a link. Raazdar's Safe Media shield checks what every file really is, not what its name says. Disguised apps are blocked, other apps get a clear warning, and every photo is rebuilt from its pixels so nothing can hide inside it. From people you haven't added, photos stay hidden, files stay locked and links are flagged until you accept the chat. All of this happens on your phone; our server never sees your files.

What is a safety number, and how does it protect my chats and calls?

Every Raazdar ID is made from its owner's secret key, so our server can't quietly swap in a fake. The safety number protects against the other risk: being given the wrong ID in the first place. Open a chat, tap ⋮ → Verify safety number, and compare the 60 digits with your contact in person or on a video call. If they match, tap Mark as verified and a ✓ appears by their name. During every call, both phones also show a 4-picture call code made from that call's encryption keys. Read the pictures to each other: if they're the same, no one is in the middle of the call.

Can Raazdar tell if a scammer is watching my screen?

On Android 15 and newer, the Raazdar app warns you the moment your screen is being recorded or shared, and it blacks out its own screen anyway. The Security tab also warns about remote-control apps (like AnyDesk or TeamViewer) and apps that can read and tap your screen, which scammers and banking malware use. This check happens only on your phone.

How big a file can I send?

Up to 25 MB, including videos. Files over 1 MB are encrypted on your phone and parked on our server, which can't open them, until your contact downloads them. They are deleted after 7 days.

Is there a desktop app?

Yes, for Windows, Mac and Linux. It blocks screenshots of its window and only ever opens Raazdar. You can also simply use Raazdar in any modern browser.

Has Raazdar been independently audited?

Not yet. Our code is public and goes through regular internal security testing (OWASP and VAPT), and we publish the results. An independent audit by an outside firm is planned, and we will publish its report in full.

How do I delete my account?

Open Raazdar → You → Privacy & your data → Withdraw consent & erase account. Your keys and chats are wiped from the device, your ID is removed from our server, and your contacts' copies can be erased as well.
