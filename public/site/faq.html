FAQ

Questions & answers

Can't find what you need? Contact us.

Do I really not need a phone number?

Yes. GhostChat never asks for a phone number, email, name or contacts. When you sign up, your phone creates a random ID and a set of encryption keys. That ID is all anyone, including us, ever sees.

Can GhostChat read my messages?

No. Messages are encrypted on your phone and can only be decrypted on the recipient's phone. Our server only ever holds sealed envelopes it cannot open, and it deletes them once they're delivered (or after 7 days).

What happens if I forget my passcode?

Nobody can recover it, not even us. That's the price of real privacy. Keep an encrypted backup (You → Encrypted backup) somewhere safe; you can restore it with your passcode on any device.

Is it free?

Yes. GhostChat has no ads and does not sell or share data. There's nothing to sell.

How is this different from other private messengers?

Most private messengers still tie your account to a phone number. GhostChat doesn't, hides the sender from the server (sealed sender), uses post-quantum encryption, and adds protection for people at risk: a panic wipe, duress passcode, auto-erase, calculator disguise and a decoy account.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone, open GhostChat in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. On Android, install the app or use the browser. It also works on any computer. See Download.

What does the panic button do?

Holding the lock button for two seconds (or typing your duress passcode) erases your keys, chats and settings from the device instantly, and can tell your contacts' apps to erase your conversations too. It cannot be undone.

Who can see that I use GhostChat?

On the normal web address, your internet provider can see you connect to quietline.chat (not what you send). For maximum anonymity use our Tor address, which hides your IP address from everyone, including us. See Security.

Is GhostChat legal to use in India?

Yes. Private, encrypted communication is lawful. Using GhostChat for anything illegal is not, and is prohibited by our Terms. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Act, 2000.

How do I delete my account?

Open GhostChat → You → Privacy & your data → Withdraw consent & erase account. Your keys and chats are wiped from the device, your ID is removed from our server, and your contacts' copies can be erased as well.
