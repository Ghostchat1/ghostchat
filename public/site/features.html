Features

A complete messenger.
Built to forget.

Everything you use every day, rebuilt so that nothing about you is collected, kept or exposed.

Messaging

Everything you'd expect. Nothing you'd regret.

Rich, fast messaging, with every message, photo, file and voice note end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your phone.

  • Replies, reactions and starred messages
  • Voice messages and documents up to 2 MB
  • Photos cleaned of location, camera and time data
  • Groups where the server can't even tell a group exists
  • Search, pin and filter chats
Identity

Your identity is a random ID. Nothing more.

No phone number, no email, no contact upload. Share your QR code or invite link only with people you choose.

  • 16-character random ID made on your device
  • A unique pattern avatar instead of a photo
  • Safety numbers to verify a contact in person
  • Nickname optional, seen only inside encrypted messages
Protection

Built for the moment things go wrong.

Designed with journalists, lawyers and anyone under pressure in mind.

  • Panic: hold the lock two seconds to erase everything
  • Duress passcode that silently wipes instead of unlocking
  • Auto-erase if the app isn't opened for days
  • Remote erase of your chats from contacts' phones
  • Calculator disguise and a believable decoy account
  • Disappearing messages and view-once photos

The details that matter

Encrypted backup

Move to a new phone with one backup and your passcode. The backup is useless to anyone else.

Tor hidden service

Use our .onion address to hide your IP address from everyone, including us.

No trackers, no cookies, no third-party code. The website and app contain none.

QR invites

Works everywhere
