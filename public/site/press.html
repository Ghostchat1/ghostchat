About GhostChat (boilerplate)

GhostChat is a private messenger from {{COMPANY}}, Ludhiana, that requires no phone number, email or name. Messages are end-to-end encrypted with hybrid post-quantum cryptography and sealed sender, so even GhostChat's own server cannot read them or see who sent them. It adds protections for people at risk, including a panic wipe, duress passcode, calculator disguise and decoy account, and is built to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
