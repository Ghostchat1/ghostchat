Raazdar is built in Ludhiana by {{COMPANY}} for anyone who needs to talk without being watched.

Why we built Raazdar

Almost every messenger starts by asking for your phone number, which in India is tied to your name, your ID documents and your bank. We think private conversation shouldn't require an identity at all. So we built a messenger that never asks who you are, can't read what you say, and can erase itself in two seconds.

Our promises

  • No ads. Ever. Not now, not “once we scale”.
  • We never sell, rent or share personal data. We hold almost none, and we'll keep it that way.
  • No trackers, analytics or third-party code on the website or in the app.
  • We will never ask for your phone number, email or name to use Raazdar.
  • We will never quietly weaken encryption. If a law ever forced a change, we would say so publicly before it took effect.
  • The personal app stays free. Core messaging, encryption and safety features will never sit behind a paywall.
  • We publish every government request we receive in our transparency report.

How we make money

We earn from paid extras and organisations, never from your data. Paid plans use the same encryption as the free app, and none of them gives us access to anyone's messages. See pricing.
