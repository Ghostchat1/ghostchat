1. Who we are

GhostChat is operated by {{COMPANY}}, {{ADDRESS}} (“we”, “us”). For your personal data we are the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”). You are the Data Principal.

2. Personal data we process and why

GhostChat is designed to process as little personal data as possible. This is the complete list:

We do not collect: your name, phone number, email address (unless you write to us), address book, location, device identifiers, advertising IDs, analytics or browsing history. Message contents are end-to-end encrypted and cannot be read by us.

Your anonymous ID is random and is not linked to you by us. We still treat it, and your public keys, as personal data and protect them accordingly.

3. Consent and legal basis

We process the data above on the basis of your consent (DPDP Act, section 6), which you give on the sign-in page or in the app before an account is created. Your consent is limited to the purposes in section 2. We may also process data where the law permits without consent (DPDP Act, section 7), for example to comply with a court order or a legal obligation.

We keep a record of the version of this notice you agreed to and when, linked to your anonymous ID, so that we can demonstrate consent as the DPDP Act requires.

4. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Information (section 11): a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities.
  • Correction and erasure (section 12): correct, complete, update or erase your personal data. Most of it lives only on your device, where you control it directly.
  • Grievance redressal (section 13): a readily available way to raise a complaint with us.
  • Nomination (section 14): nominate a person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.

How to exercise them: email {{EMAIL}} with your anonymous ID. Because we don't know who you are, we will ask you to prove that the ID is yours from inside the app before acting. We respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within 90 days, as the DPDP Rules, 2025 require.

5. Withdrawing consent and erasing your data

Open GhostChat → You → Privacy & your data → Withdraw consent & erase account. This immediately erases your keys and chats from your device, removes your ID and keys from our server, deletes messages waiting for you, and (if you choose) asks your contacts' apps to erase your conversations. Withdrawal doesn't affect processing done before it. Without an account, the service can't be provided.

6. Grievances and the Data Protection Board

Contact our Grievance Officer, {{GRIEVANCE_NAME}}, at {{EMAIL}} or {{ADDRESS}}. Details are also on our contact page. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days (Information Technology Rules, 2021), and never later than 90 days (DPDP Rules, 2025).

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India after using our grievance process (DPDP Act, section 13(3)).

7. Children

GhostChat is only for people aged 18 or older. Everyone must confirm this before creating an account. We do not knowingly process children's personal data and do not carry out tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising of anyone. If you believe a child is using GhostChat, contact us and we will help erase the account.

8. Security and breaches

We protect personal data with reasonable security safeguards (DPDP Act, section 8(5)), including end-to-end and post-quantum encryption, sealed sender, a memory-only server, encrypted on-device storage, strict access controls and regular security testing. See Security.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as the DPDP Rules require. Because we don't know who our users are, we will notify you inside the app and on this website.

9. Service providers and data location

Our server is hosted by DigitalOcean in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany, which processes data on our behalf as a Data Processor. Domain and TLS certificates are provided by our domain registrar and Let's Encrypt. Android app downloads are served by GitHub. We do not sell or share personal data with anyone for their own purposes. Transfers outside India are made in line with section 16 of the DPDP Act.

10. Cookies and tracking

We use no cookies, analytics or trackers, on the website or in the app. The app stores its encrypted data in your browser's or phone's local storage only so that it can work. That data never leaves your device unless you make a backup.

11. Requests from authorities

We comply with valid legal orders under Indian law. Because of how GhostChat is built, we hold very little: we cannot provide message contents, sender identities, contact lists or IP logs, because we do not have them.

12. Changes and languages

If we change this notice in a way that affects how we process your data, we will update the version number and ask for your consent again in the app. This notice is available in English and Hindi (हिन्दी). To request it in another language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution, email us.

The DPDP Act and DPDP Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in phases, with most obligations applying from May 2027. GhostChat already follows them.
