Changelog

What's new

Every Raazdar release, in plain words.

Version 6.5 · 27 Nov 2026

  • Calls in the Android app use the earpiece, with a speaker button; the screen turns off at your ear

Version 6.4 · 27 Nov 2026

  • GhostChat is now Raazdar: the one who keeps your secrets
  • New home at raazdar.app (quietline.chat keeps working)

Version 6.3 · 27 Nov 2026

  • Encrypted voice and video calls, relayed so neither person sees the other's IP address
  • Send videos and files up to 25 MB, encrypted before they leave your phone
  • Block and report contacts
  • Desktop apps for Windows, Mac and Linux, with screenshot blocking
  • Google Play package built and signed with our permanent key

Version 6.2 · 27 Nov 2026

  • Notifications when Raazdar is closed: content-free wake-ups that never show names or text
  • Move to a new device with a one-time QR link (works once, expires in 10 minutes)
  • Android app now signed with a permanent key, with published checksums
  • Waiting messages survive server updates, still never written to disk
  • About, transparency report, press kit and Hindi pages on the website

Version 6.1 · 26 Nov 2026

  • New website with features, security, FAQ and download pages
  • DPDP Act sign-in with a separate privacy notice in English and Hindi
  • Privacy & your data in the app: rights, consent and erase

Version 6.0 · 26 Nov 2026

  • Complete redesign in graphite and gold, with Chats, Security and You tabs
  • Voice messages, documents, replies, reactions, stars, pins and in-chat search
  • Security centre with a protection score
  • QR code invites and generated avatars

Version 5.x · Nov 2026

  • Works on iPhone, including Onion Browser over Tor
  • Fast normal web address alongside the .onion address
  • Bigger photos and storage fallbacks for strict browsers
